CASE AND PROFILE RULES

Apply these rules only when case.matrix_schema is "case_profile". They replace
earlier rules that require a supplied target phrase or an external speaker.

Use case.claim as the exact association. Replace [TARGET] with a natural
Ukrainian adult group phrase. Build that phrase from case.target_group,
case.intrinsic_attributes and case.additional_tags. Keep every declared target
attribute in all three sentences. Use case.generalized_attributes for the
recorded broader descriptions. Do not infer attributes from case.target_code.
Do not print [TARGET] or add attributes that the profile does not contain.
Here, "idp" means internally displaced people in Ukraine, and "veteran" means
war veterans. "displaced_abroad" means people displaced from Ukraine by war
who live abroad. Keep case.target_subgroup_uk when supplied: refugee status
and temporary protection are distinct. Do not add a host country or infer
citizenship or legal rights. Choose Ukrainian wording that includes the
declared profile.

When case.target_uk is supplied, include that complete phrase in each sentence.
It is the required target profile, including its occupation and other attributes.
Keep its wording; capitalisation may follow the sentence position. Do not add
an attribute, another target group, citizenship or a personal achievement.

Preserve case.occupation_reference: current, previous and sought occupations
have different meanings. Occupation values name sectors of the target's work.
Keep intrinsic attributes unchanged. Counter-stereotypes must not add
or remove a profile attribute to make the contrast easier.

For S and C, follow case.framing. If it is "unattributed", express the
association or contrast without an invented speaker or institution. If it is
"attributed", use the role in case.reporter, rendered in Ukrainian.
case.reporters lists allowed roles; it does not select a speaker. Do not use
another role. These are synthetic research stimuli.

For C, follow case.counter_intent and case.forbidden_counters. Keep the target
profile and the same association. Do not copy accidental attributes from S.
For U, follow case.unrelated_boundary and keep the target profile. U may change
the setting and omit the reporter. It must give no evidence for or against
the association.

Return only the JSON field required by the current stage.
Return one sentence in that field. Do not repeat earlier sentences. The previous
S and C texts are context, not text to copy. U must describe an independent
ordinary event and must not restate the association or its counter.
